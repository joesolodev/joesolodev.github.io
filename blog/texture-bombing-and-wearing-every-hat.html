Texture Bombing, and Wearing Every Hat

It doesn't matter how good or how seamless your material is. Repeat it past a certain point and you will notice the tiling.

Today is day 84 of my move from AAA game development to indie, and I'm working on getting texture-bombing features into the game.

Why tiling always shows

Environments tile quite a bit. It doesn't really matter how good the material is that you made in Substance Designer or wherever, or how seamless it is. When it's repeated past a certain point, you will notice it.

What you need is a technique usually called texture bombing. There are other techniques, like decals, but texture bombing is one thing that can really help break things up.

It does a lot of different things. It usually works on a hex grid instead of a square grid, so it smooths things out in a hexagonal pattern. That's what I'm working on getting into the game today.

Everybody wears a lot of hats

Normally I'm just a character artist. But since going indie and working on my own project with a three-man team, everybody has to wear a lot of hats.

I'm the character artist, the environment artist, the animator and the concept artist. I'm whatever I have to be, and so are the other two members.

More from the rest of the journey soon. Check back here for regular blog updates.

Work hard, don't suck.
