Be a Gamer First

One of my most important rules as a AAA game dev is to be a gamer. Be a gamer first, and actually play games. New games, retro games, whatever it is.

I can't stop playing video games

I got into video games because I have a habit. I've been in video games for 20 years because I can't stop playing them.

GameCube, Xbox, PS2, PS1, N64, Sega Genesis, PS3, PS4, PS5, Steam Deck, Steam Machine. I have all these things. I have tons of ROMs. I've got my own freaking Game Pass.

I am a gamer first, before anything. It's a big reason I wanted to go indie.

AAA stopped caring about core gamers

AAA has gone in a direction where I don't really think they care about the core gamers anymore. They just care about turning a profit, which is fine, I guess. It's not that big of a deal.

But as a gamer, I'm looking for a certain level of service. I'm looking for a certain level of game. That doesn't necessarily mean AAA quality. It just means depth and fun factor.

Most devs don't play games

The reason this is happening is that most devs now do not play a lot of games. They do not care. They don't play anything new that comes out. They don't play anything old. They don't really play anything.

For a lot of them, I actually don't understand why they're there. They don't like games. They don't like gamers. They don't really like anything that has to do with the industry. I think they just can't find a job in film, so they're "settling" for video games, and that's really messing things up.

No one should be a game developer who isn't a gamer, and no one should be a game developer who doesn't actually enjoy playing video games.

Work hard, don't suck.
