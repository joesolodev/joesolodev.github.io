Grand Theft Auto 6 is the last handmade game of its kind and depth that will ever be made. If you don't understand what this game is, you haven't been paying attention.

## A pre-AI game in a post-AI world

Grand Theft Auto 6 was developed in a pre-AI world and has now come out in a post-AI world.

The entire development of Grand Theft Auto 7, whenever it starts, will be done in an environment that uses AI.

## A capstone to generations of game development

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a capstone to generations of video game development, the last 30 or 40 years of it. Games will never be made quite like this ever again.

That's how much AI has changed the landscape.

## The last of its kind

Things are now forever changed. Nothing will ever be done the same.

Grand Theft Auto 6 will go down in history as the last handmade, hand-authored and truly hand-crafted experience from hundreds, from thousands of developers.
